SG Evidence Intelligence / In preparation

Evidence for a Singapore AI decision.

Research service in preparation

Source-backed evidence briefs on Singapore AI programmes, deployment and governance.

Make the public record useful.

SG Evidence Intelligence is a service project within Superintelligence SG. It prepares concise evidence briefs for people working through a defined AI decision in Singapore.

It is useful when a team needs to understand a public programme, frame deployment choices or identify governance questions before taking the next step. It does not represent a registered company, certification body or ranking service.

01 / Public record

Start with sources.

Begin with primary public material, name the source and distinguish a published claim from an open question.

02 / Review

Trace the evidence.

Link the relevant source, record its date and make the limits of the available evidence visible.

03 / Brief

Clarify the decision.

Bring the public record, assumptions and next questions into a focused brief for the decision at hand.

Public sample briefs.

These samples show the format and evidence boundary. They use public sources already cited on Superintelligence SG and are not client work, supplier rankings or outcome claims.
