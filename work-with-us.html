SG Evidence Intelligence / In preparation

Turn a hard question into a brief.

Research service in preparation

A clear route for teams who need to understand an AI decision, compare public evidence or define what to test next.

Start with the decision you need to make.

SG Evidence Intelligence is a service project within the Superintelligence SG initiative. We are preparing source-backed research briefs around real questions in Singapore’s AI ecosystem.

We do not present this project as an established company, certification body or ranking service. The useful first step is a specific question and the context around it.

01 / Question

Name the decision.

What are you trying to choose, explain, compare or prepare for?

02 / Evidence

Show the constraints.

Share the sources, timing, people affected and unknowns that matter.

03 / Output

Define useful.

Tell us what would help: a short brief, comparison, source map or next-step questions.
